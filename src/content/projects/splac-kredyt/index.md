## Project Overview

**Spłać Kredyt** (Pay Off the Mortgage) is a tool designed to calculate the best way to overpay a mortgage in order to maximize financial benefits. It allows users to run detailed simulations of various repayment and investment scenarios.

## Key Features

1. **Recurring Overpayments** – Ability to plan regular, monthly overpayments to reduce the principal.
2. **One-time Overpayments** – Entering single cash injections at specific points during the mortgage period.
3. **Variable Interest Rate (WIBOR) Strategies** – Simulating various macroeconomic scenarios: cyclical changes, long-term decreases, increases, and other interest rate fluctuations.
4. **Comparison with Investing** – A built-in analytical module that answers the question: "should I overpay the mortgage or invest a fixed amount?", visualizing the differences in profits and costs between these two strategies.
5. **Repayment Schedule Table** – A detailed breakdown of paid installments, interest, and remaining principal, taking into account all specified parameters on a timeline.
